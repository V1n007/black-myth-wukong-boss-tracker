Privacy Policy for Wukong Boss Tracker
Last Updated: December 29, 2025
Overview
Wukong Boss Tracker (“the App”) is a free, offline boss checklist application for the game Black Myth: Wukong. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.
Developer Information
Developer: V1n007Contact Email: shawlshade@gmail.com App Name: Wukong Boss Tracker
Data Collection
The App does NOT collect, transmit, or share any personal data.
The App operates entirely offline and does not:
	∙	Collect personal information
	∙	Track user behavior
	∙	Use analytics services
	∙	Display advertisements
	∙	Connect to the internet
	∙	Require user accounts or registration
Data Storage
All data created by the App (boss completion status, notes, and progress) is stored locally on your device only. This data includes:
	∙	Boss checkmarks (completed/incomplete status)
	∙	Custom notes you add for each boss
	∙	Your overall progress statistics
This data:
	∙	Never leaves your device
	∙	Is not backed up to any cloud service
	∙	Is not accessible to the developer or any third parties
	∙	Will be deleted if you uninstall the App
Third-Party Services
The App does not use any third-party services, including:
	∙	Analytics (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics)
	∙	Advertising networks
	∙	Crash reporting tools
	∙	Social media integrations
	∙	Cloud storage services
Permissions
The App may request the following permissions:
	∙	Storage/File Access (if applicable): Only used to save your local progress data on your device
These permissions are used solely for the App’s core functionality and do not enable any data collection.
Children’s Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect any information from children or adults. It is safe for users of all ages as it operates entirely offline with no data collection.
Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on the GitHub repository. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
Data Security
Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device’s security measures. We recommend:
	∙	Keeping your device’s operating system updated
	∙	Using device encryption
	∙	Using a secure lock screen
Your Rights
Since the App does not collect any personal data, there is no data for you to access, modify, or delete from our systems. You have complete control over your data by managing the App on your device:
	∙	To reset your progress: Clear app data in settings or uninstall and reinstall
	∙	To delete all data: Uninstall the App
